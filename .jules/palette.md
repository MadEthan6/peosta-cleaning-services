## 2026-08-31 - Adding accessibility attributes to icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** When adding aria-label and title to icon-only buttons, it is critical to also add aria-hidden="true" to the SVG icon itself, and be careful not to introduce massive unintended formatting changes during the process.\n**Action:** Only format the specific lines touched or avoid global formatters that might trigger large diffs when fixing small UX/a11y issues.
