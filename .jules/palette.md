## 2024-05-18 - Tooltips for icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** When adding `aria-label`s to icon-only buttons for accessibility, adding `title` attributes provides immediate visual UX feedback via tooltips for mouse users, and `aria-hidden="true"` prevents redundant screen reader announcements of the SVG itself.\n**Action:** Always include all three attributes (`aria-label`, `title`, and `aria-hidden="true"` on the SVG) together whenever implementing icon-only buttons.
